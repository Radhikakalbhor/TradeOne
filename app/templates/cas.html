About NationalDepo CAS

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) provides a single, unified view of all investments across all demat accounts held by an investor across different brokers.

  • Consolidates holdings across all linked Depository Participants.
  • Lists ISINs, security descriptions, free units, and pledged/locked units.
  • Itemizes portfolio market valuations as of current closing prices.
  • Includes full transaction debit/credit history for the selected timeframe.
  • Encrypted with 128-bit AES password protection.
